## question
Mia has seven apples. She gives three apples to her friend. How many apples does Mia have left?

## counting
Let's count Mia's apples. One, two, three, four, five, six, seven.

## give
Now Mia gives three apples away. One, two, three.

## keep
Look at the apples that stay. One, two, three, four.

## answer
Seven take away three is four. Mia has four apples left. Great job!
