## hook
Hello, math explorers! Today we're solving seven plus five. Are you ready?

## sevens
First, we have seven delicious red apples. Let's count them! One, two, three, four, five, six, seven.

## fives
Now, let's add five more apples. Here come the green ones! One, two, three, four, five. How many apples do we have altogether?

## tens
Seven needs three more to make ten. We take three from our five apples. Now we have ten, with two left over. Ten plus two equals twelve!

## win
Great job! Seven plus five equals twelve! Look at all twelve apples.

## recap
Let's remember how we did it. Seven plus five is the same as seven plus three plus two. Seven plus three is ten, and ten plus two is twelve. Remember, making ten helps us add numbers more easily!
